TITLE: Obesity Pharmacotherapy Protocol (Local)
1) Initiation: GLP-1 may be started in adults with BMI ≥30 or ≥27 with comorbidity.
2) Stop rule: Discontinue at 12 weeks if <5% weight loss unless specialist override.
3) Peri-op: Hold weekly GLP-1 one week prior to general anesthesia or deep sedation.
