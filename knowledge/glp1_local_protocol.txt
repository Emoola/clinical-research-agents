Local GLP-1 protocol (adult):
- Start low and titrate q4 weeks; monitor GI tolerance, hydration, and glucose.
- Hold 1 week prior to elective procedures requiring anesthesia.
- Avoid in medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN2 history; caution in pancreatitis history.
- Follow-up at 4 and 12 weeks; discontinue if <5% weight loss by 12 weeks unless medically justified.
